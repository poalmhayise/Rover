YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy & payment information

Hired is a manually approved document-preparation service. Please read these details before placing an order.

Information we collect

We collect the profile, education, employment and contact information you enter in the document form; your order details and unique access token; and any payment proof you submit. This information is needed to prepare your CV or application letter and identify your payment.

How payments are processed

Customers transfer money directly to the FNB bank account shown at checkout. Hired does not collect or store bank passwords or online banking login credentials. Proof-upload does not verify a payment. The site owner manually checks actual funds received in the FNB bank account before approving a PDF download. The universal price is US$0.60 USD per document. Bank fees are separate and may exceed the price; small foreign-currency transfers may be unavailable. The receiving FNB account must be confirmed eligible for inward USD payments before checkout can open.

How documents are delivered

After the owner verifies and approves payment, the ordered PDF is available through a private order link. Anyone who has the link's secret token may access that PDF. Keep your private link confidential.

Where data is stored

Order information and uploaded payment documents are kept in the website's private database and file storage. The operator should protect the hosting account, use HTTPS, restrict admin access, make backups, and delete information when no longer needed. Customers may request deletion through the website owner's published support contact.

Service limits

The user is responsible for the accuracy and truthfulness of information they submit. Hired supplies formatted documents, not a guarantee of interviews or employment. Payment confirmation depends on bank transfer settlement and a manual check, not instant automated processing.

Before public launch

The site owner should publish a support address, data retention period, refunds/cancellations policy, and business contact details, and confirm applicable local privacy and consumer requirements. This page is an initial disclosure, not legal advice.
